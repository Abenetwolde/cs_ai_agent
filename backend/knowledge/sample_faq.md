# Sample support knowledge (replace before using for a real company)

This repository is a starter demo and does not represent a real store's terms. The examples below are fictional demo policies. Replace them with approved, current company content before inviting customers to use the assistant.

## Order tracking

Customers can ask for an order update by providing their order ID. The demo lookup recognizes ADDIS-1001 and ADDIS-1002 only. ADDIS-1001 is marked shipped with Ethio Post and a demo estimated delivery date of 2026-11-24. ADDIS-1002 is processing and has no estimated delivery date yet. The demo cannot access any other order records. Never ask a customer to share a password, payment card number, or one-time code.

## Returns and refunds

Return window, eligible items, return shipping costs, and refund timing have not been configured for this demo. Do not promise that an item can be returned or give a refund timeline. Tell the customer that the return policy is not available yet and offer to flag the request for a support representative.

## Account support

The demo cannot access or change customer accounts. Never request passwords, payment card information, or authentication codes. For an account change, account access problem, suspected fraud, or private account question, direct the customer to a verified company support channel or flag the conversation for staff follow-up.

## Product information

No product catalogue, pricing, warranty, or compatibility information has been configured for this demo. If a question needs those details, say the information is not available and do not guess.
