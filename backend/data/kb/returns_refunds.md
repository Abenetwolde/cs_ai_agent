# Returns and refunds

This starter policy is sample content and must be approved and replaced by the company before customer use. Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery if the item is unused and in its original packaging. Perishable, personalized, and final-sale products are not eligible. The support team provides a return label after eligibility is confirmed. Refunds go to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after the returned item is inspected. Never promise a refund before inspection.
